How Quakers use queries

Asking questions is central to Quakers' spiritual practices. Since Quaker meetings don't have ministers, we have a practice of considering a set of questions together as a meeting. We sit together, or connect remotely, for about an hour. The month's queries (questions) are read, then we sit in silence. Friends who have a response speak that into the silence. These discussions help us share with each other how we are doing spiritually, physically, mentally. In my meeting there are twelve sets of queries, organized by topic.     Dynamic queries and responses

    Our queries are meant to be asked again and again. But we have to guard against answers becoming routine. And the questions themselves can become dated. What often brings a query back to life is a story. When I can connect a query to something that actually happened, a new question usually rises out of the story. I've begun calling these dynamic queries.
